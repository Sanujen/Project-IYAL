Project IYAL — 11-Step Normalization Architecture

📄 Read Our Research Paper on ResearchGate
  1. Paragraph Input: Accept multi-sentence Tamil/Tanglish/Legacy/English text or uploaded .docx, .txt, and .pdf files.
  2. Sentence & Token Segmentation: Segment text by sentence boundaries (preserving URLs, emails, and brackets) and tokenize word by word.
  3. 5-Type Token Taxonomy: Distinguish Raw Tamil Unicode, Mixed Tamil+English Unicode, English Corpus/Protected words, Legacy Font Encoding, and Romanized Tanglish.
  4. Unicode Script Classification: Inspect codepoints (U+0B80–U+0BFF) for fast initial categorization.
  5. English & Protected Corpus Check: Match non-Tamil tokens against the cached NLTK English corpus (including morphological inflections) and user-supplied Protected Words.
  6. Trained Legacy vs. Romanized Classifier (Model 1): Non-English ASCII tokens are classified by our fine-tuned BERT classifier (sanujen/Tamil_Legacy_Roman_Classifier_V2).
  7. Legacy Font Detection & UI Confirmation: Legacy tokens are matched across 25 Tamil font encodings (Bamini, Anjal, TSCII, etc.) via encode2unicode and confirmed by the user in the UI.
  8. Transliteration: Non-legacy Romanized Tamil tokens are transliterated to Tamil Unicode.
  9. Sentence & Paragraph Restructuring: Normalized tokens are reassembled with original punctuation.
  10. Optional English Translation: Contiguous English words are translated into Tamil if requested (while skipping user-protected words).
  11. Optional Colloquial-to-Standard Tamil Standardizer (Model 2): Sentences are standardized into formal written Tamil via our fine-tuned mBART model (sanujen/mBART_Tamil_Colloquial_to_Standard).
